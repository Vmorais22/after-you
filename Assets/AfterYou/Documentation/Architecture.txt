AFTER YOU - FOUNDATION ARCHITECTURE

The specification is the source of truth. This foundation intentionally contains no full story.

1. Composition root
GameBootstrapper registers all focused services before initializing them. Managers communicate
through interfaces, the service registry, and ScriptableObject event channels instead of static
singletons or scene lookups. This keeps dependencies explicit and makes individual systems
replaceable.

2. Data versus behavior
Characters, routines, dialogue, quests, narrative events, portals, time configuration, and event
channels are assets. Runtime components execute those definitions. Content can therefore grow
without adding branches to managers or coupling logic to scenes and art.

3. Narrative rules
Narrative events use generic requirement and consequence records. Days, time segments, flags,
abilities, character availability, relationships, quests, and future content can be composed in
data. StoryManager owns world-state facts; EventManager only evaluates and applies event data.

4. Managers
The managers requested by the specification are retained as small domain services. No global
manager coordinates every feature. SaveManager discovers save participants and TimeManager raises
day-end events, so autosave does not create a Time-to-Save dependency.

5. World and portals
MainStreet and interiors share one loaded scene. Paired PortalEndpoint components teleport the
player between already-rendered spaces, avoiding loading screens and fades. Presentation can later
replace the placeholder geometry without changing portal logic.

6. Generated foundation content
The editor content generator creates reusable GameSystems, Player, NPC, and Portal prefabs, all
baseline ScriptableObject assets, and the MainStreet scene. It is versioned and idempotent so the
same content can be rebuilt safely from Tools > After You > Rebuild Foundation Content.
